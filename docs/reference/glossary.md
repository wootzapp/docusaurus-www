## Terms

- **EnvActor**: Ray actor that owns one environment instance and the emulator CDP connection.
- **ChromiumRL**: Browser-engine reward signals captured via CDP in WootzApp.
- **GRPO**: Group Relative Policy Optimization (grouped rollouts with per-group advantage centering).
